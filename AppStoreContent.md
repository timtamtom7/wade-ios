### Full Description

**Your money, over text.**

Wade is the budget tracker you never have to open. You text your expenses. Wade parses them, logs them, and sends you a weekly summary every Friday.

No app to open. No categories to pick. No spreadsheets.

---

**How it works:**

Send a text like:
- "£12.50 lunch"
- "£40 groceries"
- "salary"

Wade understands the amount, figures out the category, and logs it instantly. No forms, no menus, no friction.

---

**Every Friday at 6pm:**

Wade texts you a summary:
- Total spent this week
- Total income
- Net (and whether you're winning)

---

**Pick your plan:**

**Free** — 20 expenses/month, basic parsing, this week's total

**Track ($3.99/mo)** — Unlimited expenses, weekly summaries, category insights

**Overview ($7.99/mo)** — Everything in Track, plus monthly reports, budget goals, CSV/PDF export, and multiple currencies

---

**Why Wade?**

Most budget apps feel like work. You have to open them, categorize every expense, remember what you spent. Wade removes all that friction. You already know what you spent — you just text it.

It's the budget tracker for people who hate budget trackers.
